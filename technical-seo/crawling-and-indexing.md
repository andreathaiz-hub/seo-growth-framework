## Crawling and Indexing Framework

My approach to crawling and indexing focuses on ensuring that search engines prioritize high-intent and revenue-driving pages.

### Tools Used
- Google Search Console
- Screaming Frog
- Log file analysis (when available)

### Key Checks
- Index coverage validation (submitted vs indexed)
- Identification of non-indexable or low-value URLs
- Crawl depth analysis for priority pages
- Internal linking signals supporting indexation

### Typical Issues Identified
- Parameter-based duplicate URLs
- Orphan or under-linked commercial pages
- Improper noindex usage
- Crawl budget wasted on low-value URLs

### SEO Decision Criteria
Indexation decisions are always tied to business intent:
- Pages with commercial or lead-generation intent are prioritized
- Informational pages must support internal linking or demand capture
- Low-value or redundant pages are excluded from indexation
