# SEO Growth Framework

This repository showcases how I approach SEO as a scalable, execution-focused growth channel, with a strong emphasis on on-page optimization, technical SEO, and search intent alignment.

It includes practical frameworks I use to turn audits and data into clear, prioritized actions that improve visibility, rankings, and long-term organic performance.

---

## What’s included

### Technical SEO
- Crawlability and indexation audits
- Site structure and internal linking logic
- SEO hygiene and site health prioritization

### Content & Search Intent
- Keyword research mapped to search intent
- Content optimization for informational and commercial queries
- Frameworks focused on relevance, clarity, and conversions

These materials reflect how I work on real client sites, without sharing sensitive or proprietary data.

---

## Selected Results (Real Projects)

### Large-scale travel website (content-heavy)

Applied on-page and technical SEO improvements across a large site focused on informational and discovery-based searches.

**Results (6 months vs previous period):**
- Organic impressions increased by ~20% (8.35M → 10M)
- Average position improved from 12.6 to 9.5
- Strong growth across non-brand, high-intent informational queries

This work expanded keyword coverage, improved semantic relevance, and strengthened overall organic visibility.

---

### Hospitality website – US market (brand-driven)

Implemented technical and on-page optimizations with a focus on structure, intent alignment, and non-brand keyword growth in a competitive market.

**Results (6 months vs previous period):**
- Average position improved significantly from 18.8 to 12.6
- Increased visibility for non-brand travel and vacation-related queries
- Stronger mid-funnel discovery despite natural brand and seasonal fluctuations

This project highlights experience working with brand-heavy sites and competitive SERPs.
