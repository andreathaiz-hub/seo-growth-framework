# SEO Growth Framework

This repository showcases how I approach SEO as a scalable growth and revenue-driving channel.

It includes practical frameworks covering:
- Technical SEO foundations
- Crawling and indexing strategy
- Search intent and keyword research
- Content optimization aligned with conversions
- SEO measurement based on business impact

This work reflects how I collaborate with product, engineering, and content teams in real-world environments using tools such as Google Search Console, Ahrefs, SEMrush, and Screaming Frog.
