# seo-growth-framework
SEO portfolio focused on technical SEO, search intent, content optimization, and organic growth driven by business impact.
## Keyword Research and Search Intent Framework

My keyword research process is built around intent modeling rather than volume alone.

### Tools Used
- Ahrefs
- SEMrush
- Google Search Console

### Process
1. Identify head terms tied to core business offerings
2. Expand into long-tail queries with commercial or high-intent value
3. Cluster keywords based on search intent
4. Map clusters to existing or new landing pages

### Intent Classification
- Transactional: Direct conversion or lead intent
- Commercial Investigation: Comparison, pricing, alternatives
- Informational: Top-of-funnel demand supporting authority

### Prioritization Logic
Keywords are prioritized based on:
- Intent strength
- Business relevance
- SERP composition
- Conversion potential over raw search volume
